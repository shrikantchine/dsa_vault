- **4 (Very Good)**: Review in 1 week

### Difficulty Modifiers:
- **Easy Topics**: Standard intervals
- **Medium Topics**: Standard intervals

## 📝 Quick Review Checklist

When reviewing a topic, ask yourself:

- [ ] Can I explain the key concepts without looking?
- [ ] Do I understand the time/space complexity?
- [ ] Can I solve basic problems using this concept?
- [ ] Do I know common variations and edge cases?
- [ ] Can I implement the algorithm/data structure from scratch?
